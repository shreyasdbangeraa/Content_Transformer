## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
On August 12, 2026 at 03:14 UTC, NovaTech Systems' Security Operations Center (SOC) detected unauthorized anomalous encryption activity across the primary regional production cluster located in Region US-East. Preliminary analysis confirms a sophisticated ransomware attack conducted by an advanced threat group tracked internally as "DarkHydra".

The threat actors gained initial foothold via a zero-day vulnerability in an unpatched legacy VPN gateway (host: vpn-edge02.novatech-internal.net, IP: 10.240.12.88). Approximately 500 server systems and virtualized endpoints across enterprise operations were compromised and encrypted with the '.hydra6' payload.

Immediate containment protocols isolated the affected subnet within 42 minutes of detection. Critical database vaults holding encrypted customer financial records were successfully segregated and show no evidence of exfiltration, though forensic data collection remains ongoing.

## 3. IMPACT ANALYSIS
- Systems Affected: 500 production servers and administrative workstations.
- Operational Downtime: Enterprise billing and internal customer support portals remained degraded for 18 hours.
- Data Exposure Assessment: No confirmed customer PII or financial vault exfiltration. 120GB of internal diagnostic telemetry was staged for exfiltration but blocked by egress firewall rules.
- Severity Level: CRITICAL (CVSS Severity Score 9.4).

## 5. REMEDIATION & STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Credential Reset: Enforce enterprise-wide mandatory credential reset across all Active Directory and Cloud IAM accounts.
2. Network Isolation & Segmentation: Permanently isolate legacy VPN appliances and migrate all perimeter access to zero-trust architecture.
3. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Mandate FIDO2 hardware security keys for all administrative and privileged access.
4. Comprehensive Log Audit: Conduct a 90-day retroactive log audit across all perimeter routers and domain controllers.
5. Air-gapped Backup Restoration: Re-image compromised host machines from immutable clean snapshots before bringing systems back online.
